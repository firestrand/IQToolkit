# Deferred modernization phase contracts

These fields form part of the rolling-wave [implementation plan](modernization-implementation-plan.md). They retain the complete later horizon while implementation tasks, approved contracts, data and engine-specific commands remain gated. They authorize no dispatch. Proposed facts are defined in the [future obligation ledger](modernization-future-facts.md); existing registered facts and native receipts keep their historical scope.

## Shared baseline, ownership and verification contract

Every phase starts from the hash-bound integrated checkout at dispatch, with original baseline60ddef6 and exact preceding phase receipts recorded. Root owns shared registers, configuration, locks and integrated verification; workers receive disjoint paths only after a reviewed packet defines stable interfaces and resources. At most three workers plus root/reviewer; SDK/build/test/native resources are serialized unless isolated explicitly. Every phase requires independent integrated review and three evidence-driven repairs per failure. Existing stopped packets retain their caps and cannot be restarted by these contracts.

For every phase, **Facts Introduced** are its selected Proposed behavior rows in the companion ledger; conditional rows remain inactive unless selected by the owner. **Facts Strengthened** are none by default: expansion must name any actual change. **Facts Protected** are all then-Active predecessor obligations and their declared evidence, plus existing synchronous/API/package contracts. New facts require exact test/oracle/data/configuration bindings, protecting evidence and semantic RED before their corresponding implementation; already-correct behavior uses an explicit Verify task. No proposed fact is activated by this document.

The common **Verification Command** is `bash eng/verify-final.sh` against the complete integrated fact/evidence register, after the phase's exact bindings and profile are expanded and reviewed. This is a declared closure gate, not a claim that today's incomplete final gate passes or certifies future engines. Each phase additionally requires actual engine/public-consumer evidence for its selected facts; its literal provider commands and environment setup are mandatory outputs of the prerequisite expansion gate. Missing commands/data/contracts block dispatch and closure.

The common **Demo/Validation Command** is `bash eng/verify-final.sh` only for the integrated register check. The user-facing demo is the phase-specific consuming-application behavior below. A separate literal demo invocation, approved inputs and fixture/environment manifests must be saved at expansion; none is invented before the public contract exists. Source probes or mocks cannot substitute for these end-to-end demos.

Every selected high-risk, security-sensitive or irreversible slice requires a named **Fact Sufficiency Review** before dispatch and at closure. It checks the **Rebuild Property**, boundary/equivalence-class coverage and meaningful mutation or seeded-defect evidence where applicable; expansion names the fact boundaries, applicable mutation/seeded-defect mechanism and measurable threshold; root records the reviewer and findings, and unresolved insufficiency blocks the slice. Existing verification tools are used where they fit; no new mutation framework is implied.

Every **Exit Criteria** includes: selected requirement-owned facts green through the real public entry point; protecting facts unchanged; full relevant default/format/analyzer/API/package/native/profile gates pass; risk-based coverage and applicable security/policy mutation evidence pass; no unresolved included data/contract/cost/access prerequisite; ATLAS and independent integrated review complete; support/migration docs truthful. Only then **stage changes for human review**, preserving unrelated work. Publication/promotion remains a separate owner action.

## Remaining M0 ownership and release confidence

**Role:** Hardening/support. **Target:** LOCAL-RM-M0 existing resource, cache/mapping and release contracts. **Dependencies:** Existing V0/V1 gates, unanswered data/coverage/policy decisions and separately authorized follow-up to stopped V1aj/V1ai final assessment. **Facts Introduced:** None by default; selected remaining normative obligations already exist in the current ledger or future ledger's M0 group. **Facts Strengthened:** Only named qualified bindings after reviewed expansion. **Facts Protected:** All historical34 rows and their unchanged semantics, then-Active facts and synchronous/public/package behavior.

**Observable outcome / demo:** Existing public sync queries/updates preserve results and correctly release owned resources across the expanded paths, with truthful complete release checks. **Additional verification gate:** Meaningful real-provider regression/ownership cases and current final evidence integrity; existing exhausted runs are historical, not retried. **Rollback:** Revert only task-owned repairs after preserving failing/equivalent receipts; restore compatible public behavior and original cache/resource ownership. No production database mutation. **Exit:** Shared closure contract plus all required M0 facts/readiness/coverage decisions; metadata/source probes cannot close M0.

## M1 query and command execution

**Role:** Capability. **Target:** LOCAL-RM-M1 genuine async query/command execution, streaming, cancellation and resource release. **Dependencies:** Complete M0; accepted additive execution/lifetime/failure contract; actually qualified driver path, authentic data and driver/platform matrix. **Facts Introduced:** MOD.M1.QUERY.IO.v1, MOD.M1.COMMAND.IO.v1, MOD.M1.CANCEL.v1, MOD.M1.STREAM.v1, MOD.M1.EARLY.DISPOSE.v1, MOD.M1.ASYNC.DISPOSE.v1, MOD.M1.BORROWED.v1, MOD.M1.SYNC.v1 and MOD.M1.ERRORS.v1 for these operations. **Facts Strengthened:** None unless named in expansion. **Facts Protected:** Shared contract, particularly retained synchronous APIs and resource behavior. Shared baseline, ownership, verification/demo commands and closure fields apply.

**Observable outcome / demo:** A consuming application runs an actual cancellable query/command, streams without full buffering, and breaks early with correct owned/borrowed resource release. **Additional verification gate:** Live pending-operation cancellation and recovery, bounded-memory results, operation-specific driver capability and early-break/failure cleanup evidence; Task.Run wrappers do not qualify. **Rollback:** Remove additive execution changes through reviewed compatible changes while preserving synchronous entry points and caller resources. **Exit:** Shared closure contract and every named execution fact; unsupported genuine async operations have explicit capability/failure behavior.

## M1 transactions, scopes, hosting and diagnostics

**Role:** Capability. **Target:** LOCAL-RM-M1 async transactions, concurrent scope ownership and offered opt-in hosting/diagnostics. **Dependencies:** M1 query/command execution; accepted transaction/scope/diagnostic/redaction contract; authentic driver/transaction/platform inputs. **Facts Introduced:** MOD.M1.TRANSACTION.v1, MOD.M1.SCOPES.v1, MOD.M1.HOSTING.v1, MOD.M1.LOGGING.v1, MOD.M1.REDACTION.v1 and MOD.M1.TRACING.v1. **Facts Strengthened:** MOD.M1.BORROWED.v1, MOD.M1.ERRORS.v1, MOD.M1.CANCEL.v1 and MOD.M1.ASYNC.DISPOSE.v1 only for the new transaction/scope surfaces, through reviewed evidence changes without weakening their semantics. **Facts Protected:** All prior execution and synchronous facts. Shared baseline, ownership, verification/demo commands and closure fields apply.

**Observable outcome / demo:** A consuming application begins/commits/rolls back supported async transactions, isolates concurrent scopes, and opts into hosting plus redacted structured logs/traces/metrics. **Additional verification gate:** Actual owned/borrowed transaction outcomes, cleanup/cancellation, separate-connection scope isolation, correct DI lifetimes and diagnostics from success/failure paths. **Rollback:** Disable/remove optional integration through compatible changes; dispose only owned resources and preserve caller transaction state. **Exit:** Shared closure contract and every named transaction/scope/diagnostic fact; integration availability is required while application use remains opt-in.

## M1q shared mapping and query features

**Role:** Capability for each selected slice; selection/deferral is a prerequisite decision. **Target:** LOCAL-RM-MAPPING / LOCAL-RM-QUERY. **Dependencies:** M0; async execution slices also M1; owner selects bounded features/workloads alongside consuming provider milestones. **Facts Introduced:** Selected future ledger M1q rows; remaining rows stay conditional with an explicit approved deferral reason. **Facts Strengthened:** None by default. **Facts Protected:** Existing mappings/query semantics and all Active predecessor facts.

**Observable outcome / demo:** The public API constructs selected immutable/record shapes or round-trips selected types, paginates deterministically, binds raw parameters, reports conflicts/partial failures or enforces selected policy behavior. **Additional verification gate:** Every listed feature selected or explicitly deferred; each consuming provider verifies its own null/missing/type/order/conflict/policy semantics. Tenant/bypass security facts need adversarial and mutation evidence. **Rollback:** Retain old mapping defaults/APIs; revert only additive feature slices. Persisted converter/cursor/policy changes require explicit compatible migration, with no silent identity or tenant change. **Exit:** Shared closure contract for selected slices; completing all features is not a prerequisite for unrelated providers.

## M3 DynamoDB

**Role:** Capability. **Target:** LOCAL-RM-M3 keyed NoSQL with explicit cost/consistency. **Dependencies:** M1; accepted keyed/index/converter/cursor/error/retry contract; approved data and disposable AWS account/region/resource/cost/cleanup scope. **Facts Introduced:** Future ledger M3 keyed/query/conditional/index/presence/pagination/budget/scan/credential/retry obligations. **Facts Strengthened:** None by default. **Facts Protected:** Shared synchronous/async contracts without importing relational transaction assumptions.

**Observable outcome / demo:** Typed keyed access and partition query preserve conditional conflicts, empty-page continuation, index consistency and bounded request/capacity behavior. **Additional verification gate:** Local contract checks plus actual AWS-specific evidence; emulation alone is insufficient. One retry owner, unsafe-write uncertainty and no implicit scan must be observable. **Rollback:** Disable/remove the optional integration while retaining native service access and application data. Cleanup only manifest-owned disposable resources; writes/cursors need compatible versioning and uncertain-outcome recovery. **Exit:** Shared closure contract and approved AWS evidence/cost disposition; no alternative-server certification from protocol similarity.

## M4a vector retrieval

**Role:** Capability, two separately accepted experiments before a stable contract. **Target:** LOCAL-RM-M4A pgvector then Qdrant. **Dependencies:** M1; pgvector additionally M2; interoperability discovery; owner-reviewed retrieval/key/type/model/score/exactness/security contract; authentic engine data and approved quality corpus. **Facts Introduced:** Future ledger M4a vector validation/filter/isolation/top-k/projection/score/cancellation/quality obligations for each engine. **Facts Strengthened:** None by default. **Facts Protected:** Existing contracts and embedding/model hosting remaining application infrastructure.

**Observable outcome / demo:** The same reviewed application request executes through both experiments with explicit capabilities and backend-specific score metadata, validation and isolation. **Additional verification gate:** Real round trips, pre-dispatch invalid-value rejection, candidate-filter semantics and adversarial isolation, live cancellation/cleanup and corpus quality; no cross-engine raw-score equivalence. **Rollback:** Disable the optional adapter and retain the authoritative data/native client path. Engine-owned indexes/collections require explicit manifest cleanup and rebuild policy; never remove unrelated records or invoke model generation silently. **Exit:** Shared closure contract and separate engine acceptance; Milvus remains separately probed and cannot be promoted automatically.

## Later extensions and compatibility discovery

**Role:** Probe for required empirical discovery; Capability only for owner-selected follow-ons. **Target:** LOCAL-RM-DISCOVERY / LOCAL-RM-LATER. **Dependencies:** Named required probes may proceed independently through bounded source/native packets; implementations require selected demand, accepted contracts and real data. **Facts Introduced:** Selected future ledger later-extension behavior rows only. **Facts Strengthened:** None unless named. **Facts Protected:** All existing registered facts and previous failed/stopped evidence; probes enable future obligations without activating product facts.

**Observable outcome / demo:** MariaDB, Milvus, vector abstraction and trimming/AOT findings receive explicit outcomes/support decisions; selected CTE/window/source-generation, DynamoDB batch/transaction/TTL/stream or index-sync use cases demonstrate their own behavior. **Additional verification gate:** Real named-version compatibility for engine claims; no protocol/API compile substitute. Source-only probe commands are in their expanded packets; future behavior uses the complete integrated gate and approved demo command. **Rollback:** Retire only owned experimental resources and preserve failures; selected schema/cursor/event changes need expand/contract/replay recovery. **Exit:** Every named discovery and conditional follow-on has a truthful outcome/selection disposition; no blanket compatibility/AOT promise.

## Final support and documentation

**Role:** Documentation/Hardening support. **Target:** LOCAL-RM-SUPPORT and complete roadmap inventory. **Dependencies:** All selected behavior gates and required discoveries; actual engine/SDK/OS evidence; owner promotion/exclusion/version decisions. **Facts Introduced:** None by default; future ledger support/process obligations are acceptance records, not activated product behavior. **Facts Strengthened:** Verified bindings and consumer examples only after reviewed equivalence. **Facts Protected:** All Active product semantics and immutable released artifacts.

**Observable outcome / demo:** Consumers can run documented examples and determine exact experimental/supported/legacy packages, versions, platforms, exclusions, migration steps and integration skips. **Additional verification gate:** Complete requirement-by-requirement fact/evidence audit, exact package consumer examples and actual support matrix; Git tags do not imply NuGet publication. **Rollback:** Correct/revert task-owned docs and withdraw unqualified support claims without modifying released bits. Public compatibility changes require approved version/migration policy. **Exit:** Shared closure contract and no unresolved required inventory item; only then may the full modernization goal be marked complete.
